Most recently, I was part of the development of a Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) system at Mycophyto under the supervision of [Audrey Lami Martinot](https://www.linkedin.com/in/audrey-lamy-martinot/?locale=en_US). This work focused on integrating structured data extraction with Large Language Models (LLMs) to retrieve domain-specific knowledge on arbuscular mycorrhizal fungi. Previously, during my undergraduate studies, I completed my thesis on NLP under the supervision of Prof. [Md Kishor Morol](https://www.linkedin.com/in/kishormorol/). I also participated in a computer vision project titled Fire Detection From Images and Videos Using YOLOv5, where we proposed an enhanced model with Prof. [Md Shakhawat Hossain](https://iub.ac.bd/academics/departments/cse/faculty-and-staff/shakhawat). Besides, I have also worked on graph algorithms with Prof. Dr. [Md Manzurul Hasan](https://www.aiub.edu/faculty-list/faculty-profile#manzurul@aiub.edu), contributing to the solution of an antimagic labeling problem. 

## Interests

-  Explainable AI, Agentic AI, Computer Vision, Natural Language Processing, Graphs Algorithms.
