I am currently in M1, pursuing my MSc in Data Science and Artificial Intelligence at Université Côte d'Azur. I received my Bachelor of Science in Computer Science and Engineering from American International University-Bangladesh [(AIUB)](https://www.aiub.edu/). 

I have been involved in several research projects across diverse domains of artificial intelligence. Throughout my academic life, I have spent time on Natural Language Processing, Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition and also on Graph Theory and Algorithms.
My primary research interests include safe super intelligence, agentic AI, computer vision, natural language processing, and graph theory and algorithms. Nonetheless, I remain open to exploring emerging areas of AI and related disciplines.


Outside of academia, I am a sport enthusiast. I enjoy outdoor activities such as football, badminton and cycling. I love photography and travelling. Besides, I enjoy discussing about theology and politics too. Feel free to discuss any of the topics if we happen to cross paths!
