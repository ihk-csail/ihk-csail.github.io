I am currently in M1, pursuing my MSc in Data Science and Artificial Intelligence at Université Côte d'Azur. I received my Bachelor of Science in Computer Science and Engineering from American International University-Bangladesh [(AIUB)](https://www.aiub.edu/). 

I have been involved in several research projects across diverse domains of artificial intelligence. Most recently, I contributed to the development of a Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) system at Mycophyto under the supervision of [Audrey Lami Martinot](https://www.linkedin.com/in/audrey-lamy-martinot/?locale=en_US). This work focused on integrating structured data extraction with Large Language Models (LLMs) to retrieve domain-specific knowledge on arbuscular mycorrhizal fungi. Previously, during my undergraduate studies, I completed my thesis on NLP under the supervision of Prof. [Md Kishor Morol](https://www.linkedin.com/in/kishormorol/). I also participated in a computer vision project titled Fire Detection From Images and Videos Using YOLOv5, where we proposed an enhanced model, Fire-YOLOv5 to detect fire-like targets in indoor, outdoor, and forest environments under varying natural lighting conditions with Prof. [Md Shakhawat Hossain](https://iub.ac.bd/academics/departments/cse/faculty-and-staff/shakhawat). These experiences have further strengthened my enthusiasm for working at the intersection of computer vision and computational linguistics.

Besides, I have also worked on graph algorithms with Prof. Dr. [Md Manzurul Hasan](https://www.aiub.edu/faculty-list/faculty-profile#manzurul@aiub.edu), contributing to the solution of an antimagic labeling problem. This experience catalyzed my interest in the intersection of graph theory and artificial intelligence, which continues to influence my current research directions.

My primary research interests include safe and aligned intelligence, agentic AI, computer vision, natural language processing, and graph theory and algorithms. Nonetheless, I remain open to exploring emerging areas of AI and related disciplines.

Outside of academia, I love photography. Besides, I am an enthusiast of sports, particularly football, badminton, and chess. I also like discuss about religion and politics. Feel free to reach out if you’d like to discuss any of these interests!
